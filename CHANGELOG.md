- **The Customer Insights Researcher learns how to know a finding is real — and stops telling you to invent a percentage** (both dual-located copies of [`analytics/analytics-customer-insights-researcher.md`](analytics/analytics-customer-insights-researcher.md); counts unchanged at 75 agents / 19 skills). Names the three misreads that cost citations: blocking `Google-Extended` does **not** remove you from AI Overviews (Google states it doesn't affect Search inclusion or ranking; `noindex`/`nosnippet`/`max-snippet`/`data-nosnippet` are what do); blocking `GPTBot` does **not** hide you from ChatGPT search (that index is `OAI-SearchBot`); and robots.txt is **not** access control for user-triggered fetchers (OpenAI notes robots.txt may not apply to `ChatGPT-User`; Perplexity documents that `Perplexity-User` generally ignores it — anything that must not be fetched belongs behind auth).
