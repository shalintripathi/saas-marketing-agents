## Copywriter's notes

- **Word count:** ~160 body words — inside the 150–300 B2B scan window; single idea per paragraph; bolded scan path tells the story alone.
- **CTA:** one primary ("Enable Incident Timeline" — specific action, not "learn more"). Reply-to is a deliberate secondary: this brand answers email.
- **Testing discipline (Rule 9):** do **not** declare a subject-line winner on open rate — privacy proxies and security scanners fetch pixels with no human involved, and a fixed "≥10% gap" is not a significance test. Decide on **clicks to the enable page** (the least-fakeable signal this send can power); if volume can't power it at 212 customers *(fictional demo metric)*, log the result as directional, not a winner.
- **Tone check:** no exclamation points, no fake excitement, no banned words. Reads like a colleague, not a marketer.
- **Prospect variant:** Hold. This send is customer activation; a prospect version should ride the blog + LinkedIn wave and route through the nurture track — `[NEEDS INPUT: decision on whether a prospect list exists and is mailable under its consent basis]`.
